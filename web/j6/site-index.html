ChoralRoot J-6

A chord instrument with the 100 chord sets of the Roland J-6's chord set list, on its own engine. Not affiliated with Roland.

Play the chord player
100 chord sets, 12 keys, phrases, arpeggios and a 16-step sequencer in the browser. Run the firmware emulator
The whole FM-1 firmware in WebAssembly: Options > Chord Set picks a set.
